About

I'm a Ph.D. student in quantitative genetics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My work combines breeding simulation with crop modeling, mostly in perennial sorghum.

I want to know what makes complex traits like perenniality work, at the level of the genome and in the field. I use stochastic models to test breeding strategies before they cost a growing season.

The goal is virtual plant populations that breeding programs can actually use, so crop models start from realistic genetics instead of averages.
